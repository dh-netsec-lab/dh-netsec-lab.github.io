## 🎯 Purpose
This section of the **Enterprise Cybersecurity Lab (ECL)** focuses on building *market-signal SOC skills*:
- Generate realistic activity (scanning, enumeration, C2)
- Detect it in Splunk
- Investigate with supporting host + network evidence
- Document triage and analyst takeaways
