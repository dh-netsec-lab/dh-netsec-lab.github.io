Enterprise Cybersecurity Lab (ECL)

Enterprise-scale cybersecurity lab focused on network security, identity services, telemetry, detection engineering, and hybrid infrastructure validation.

Overview

The Enterprise Cybersecurity Lab (ECL) is a multi-domain security engineering environment designed to simulate real-world enterprise infrastructure. It integrates firewalls, identity services, telemetry pipelines, network access control, hybrid connectivity, and security monitoring workflows to demonstrate practical design, deployment, validation, and troubleshooting skills.
