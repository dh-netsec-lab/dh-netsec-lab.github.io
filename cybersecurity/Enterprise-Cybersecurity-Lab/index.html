Enterprise Cybersecurity Lab (ECL)

“Building a living enterprise, one phase at a time.”

Vision & Purpose

The Enterprise Cybersecurity Lab is a fully built real-world simulation of an enterprise network, integrating firewalls, identity services, SIEM telemetry, AWS connectivity, NAC, IDS/IPS, and vulnerable applications — designed to demonstrate real engineering capability.
