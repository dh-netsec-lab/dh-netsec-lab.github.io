# Enterprise Cybersecurity Lab (ECL)

Welcome to the **Enterprise Cybersecurity Lab (ECL)** — a full SOC environment designed to simulate real-world enterprise security operations.

This lab integrates:

- **Suricata IDS**
- **Zeek NTA**
- **Wazuh SIEM**
- **Sysmon**
- **Splunk**
- **Windows Active Directory**
- **Firewall Telemetry & Logging**
- **Multi-phase Detection Pipeline**

---

## 🧩 ECL Lab Phases
