Enterprise Cybersecurity Lab (ECL)

“Building a living enterprise, one phase at a time.”

Vision & Purpose

The Enterprise Cybersecurity Lab (ECL) is a continuously evolving simulation of a real-world enterprise environment. It is designed to help engineers and architects explore how organizations design, secure, monitor, and govern complex infrastructures. Every phase introduces new layers of visibility, identity, detection, and governance.

The ECL emphasizes practical, real-world engineering by integrating multi-vendor firewalls, identity platforms, telemetry pipelines, logging systems, and enterprise architecture concepts. Nothing is theoretical — everything is fully implemented, validated, and documented.

Lab Overview

The ECL integrates full-stack network security, SOC engineering, and enterprise IT infrastructure. It includes:

  • Multi-site routed network (Bama • New York • Cali)
  • Palo Alto & Fortinet firewalls
  • ClearPass NAC (802.1X / TACACS+)
  • Suricata IDS & Zeek NTA
  • Windows AD, DNS, DHCP, PKI
  • Splunk SIEM, Sysmon, Rsyslog pipeline
  • AWS S2S VPN & Zscaler ZIA representation
  • Vulnerable apps (bWAPP, Metasploitable, PHP Auction)
